Questions

Good to know.

Is Gate Zone really free?

Yes. Create an account with your email and a password and everything in the studio is open to you.

Which exam is it for?

GATE Data Science & Artificial Intelligence (DA). Every subject and topic follows the DA syllabus.

Are the past papers included?

GATE 2024, 2025 and 2026 are in the library, 65 questions each, with the official answers and source notes.

Can I practise only what I am weak at?

Yes. Pick any subjects and topics, set the length, and the set is split equally across your choice. You can also filter the library by year, type, subject and topic.

Is there negative marking?

It follows the real paper: a wrong MCQ costs one third of the marks on a 1-mark question and two thirds on a 2-mark question. MSQ and NAT have no negative marking.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser. Your saved questions, sessions and progress sit in your account.
